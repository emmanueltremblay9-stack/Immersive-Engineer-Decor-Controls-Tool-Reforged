Hōʻailona Kahua Mākēneki
Hōʻailona pōʻino
<&recipe>Kaha ka Hōʻailona "E Makaʻala, Kahua Mākēneki Ikaika", Hōʻailona Kahua Mākēneki, i nā pōʻino, nā puka, a me nā wahi hana me ka hōʻailona ʻike palekana no ka halehana.<br>
<br>
Hoʻohana: E kau ma ka ʻaoʻao o nā pōʻino, nā puka, nā mīkini, a me nā wahi kapu.<br>
