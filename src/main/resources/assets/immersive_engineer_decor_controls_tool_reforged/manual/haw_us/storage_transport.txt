Mālama a me ka Lawe
Nā lako waihona a me ke kahe
Mālama nā lako waihona a lawe i nā pahu, nā palela, nā kīʻaha, a me nā kiwikā no nā ala halehana paʻapu.<br>
<br>
E hoʻohana i kēia mau ʻaoʻao i ka hoʻonohonoho ʻana i nā lumi waihona, nā ala paipu, nā pahu wai mālama, a me nā laina wai hoʻomalu ʻia.
<np>
Pahu Lepili
Lepili waihona hiki ke hoʻololi
<&labeled_crate>Kūkulu ʻia kēia pahu lepili no ka hoʻokaʻawale mōakāka i ka lumi waihona. Hiki ke hoʻololi i kona papa alo e like me ka hōʻailona hale hana liʻiliʻi.<br>
<br>
Hoʻohana: E kaomi ʻākau i ka papa lepili e hoʻololi i ka lepili pahu. E kaomi ʻākau i ke koena o ka pahu e wehe i ka waihona lako, nā ʻāpana, a me nā lako mālama.<br>
<np>
Palela Wai
Lako laina wai
<&fluid_barrel>He ʻāpana laina wai paʻapu kēia no nā pahu wai liʻiliʻi, nā ala paipu, nā wahi ʻohi, a me ke kahe i hoʻomalu ʻia e Redstone.<br>
<br>
Hoʻohana: E kau ma kahi e hiki ai nā paipu a me nā pākeke i ke alo pahu wai.<br>
<np>
