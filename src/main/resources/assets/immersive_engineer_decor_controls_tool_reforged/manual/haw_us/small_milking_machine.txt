Mīkini Waiū
Lako kōkua papahele hale hana
<&recipe>He palaka kōkua papahele hale hana ka Mīkini Waiū Liʻiliʻi no ka hanalima, ka mālama, ke kau ʻana, ka wāwahi, ka ʻohi, a i ʻole ka hana ʻakomi paʻapu.<br>

Hoʻohana: E kau kokoke i nā pipi a hoʻolako i ke ala lawe i nā ipu waiū a i ʻole ka waiū kahe.<br>
