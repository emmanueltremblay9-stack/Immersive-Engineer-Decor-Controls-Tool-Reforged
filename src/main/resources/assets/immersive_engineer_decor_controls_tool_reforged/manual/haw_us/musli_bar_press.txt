Mea Kaomi Paukū ʻAi Muslee
Mea kaomi ʻai
<&recipe>Hoʻololi ka Muslee Bar Press i nā mea ʻai maʻamau i rations liʻiliʻi no ka hana.<br>
<br>
Hoʻohana: E lawe i ka palaoa, ka ʻāpala, a me ka ʻanoʻano palaoa, melon, pumpkin, beetroot, a i ʻole Industrial Hemp, a hoʻohana i ka press. Pau hoʻokahi o kēlā me kēia mea, nānā ʻia ka hakahaka, hana ʻia ʻehā Muslee Melange Power Bar, a emi ka durability o ka press.<br>
<br>
Inā nele kekahi mea a ʻaʻohe hakahaka no nā bar, ʻaʻohe mea e pau.
