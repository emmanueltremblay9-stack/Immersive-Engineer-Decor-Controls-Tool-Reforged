Kīʻaha Wai
Lako laina wai
<&recipe>He ʻāpana laina wai paʻapu ka Kīʻaha Wai Liʻiliʻi no nā pahu wai liʻiliʻi, nā ala paipu, nā wahi ʻohi, a me ke kahe i hoʻomalu ʻia e Redstone.<br>
<br>
Hoʻohana: E kau ma lalo o kahi ʻohi wai a hoʻoili i nā mea i mālama ʻia i nā pahu wai a i ʻole nā paipu lalo.<br>
