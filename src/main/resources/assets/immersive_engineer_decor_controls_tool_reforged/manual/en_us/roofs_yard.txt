Roofs & Yard
Outdoor factory fittings
Roof blocks, yard grit, and old industrial wood parts help finish exterior sheds, work yards, loading areas, and weathered factory edges.<br>
<br>
Mix these pieces with masonry and accessways to keep outdoor industrial builds readable from a distance.

Roof Chimney Trunk
Weathered roof fitting
<&dark_shingle_roof_chimneytrunk>This roof fitting provides a raised rim for the separate Dark Shingle Roof Chimney.<br>
<br>
Usage: Aim at the rim with a chimney head to place it above the fitting. Use the head's page for smoke controls; the trunk itself is a roof fitting.<br>
<br>
New placements stay upright and straight instead of turning into roof corners. Existing corner-shaped fittings keep their old state; replace one if you need the visible chimney fitting.
<np>
Roof Wire Conduit
Weathered roof fitting
<&dark_shingle_roof_wireconduit>The Dark Shingle Roof Wire Conduit has a small opening straight through its center, from top to bottom.<br>
<br>
Usage: Align two compatible Immersive Engineering connectors vertically above and below the opening, then connect them with the matching wire coil. The conduit lets the cable pass; it is not itself a connector or relay. Off-center or sideways cables can hit the solid walls.<br>
<br>
New placements keep the fitting upright and straight. Existing straight fittings gain the hollow collision without replacement. Replace an old corner-shaped fitting to restore its visible conduit.<br>
<np>
Dark Shingle Roof
Weathered roof fitting
<&dark_shingle_roof_chimney>This chimney emits adjustable decorative smoke without fuel.<br>
<br>
Usage: Right-click to cycle the intensity from 0 to 15; 0 switches smoke off. A new unpowered chimney starts at 5. A change in incoming redstone strength sets the intensity to that signal, including 0 when power is removed.<br>
<br>
Hold another chimney to build upward instead of adjusting the smoke. In a contiguous stack of chimney heads, only the top head emits smoke.

Old Industrial Wood Door
Aged shop timber
<&old_industrial_wood_door>This wooden door can open by hand and can share a manual click with a matching neighboring door.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place two doors of this type side by side at the same height, facing the same direction, with opposite hinges. With neither door powered, right-click either using an empty main hand to open or close both.<br>
<br>
The linked click respects interaction permissions. It does not add shared redstone control: powered doors keep their normal independent behavior.
