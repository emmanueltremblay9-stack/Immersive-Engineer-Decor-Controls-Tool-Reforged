Material Storage Box
Construction supply box
<&recipe>The Material Storage Box is a portable 54-slot inventory for construction runs. It preserves arbitrary item stacks and their components.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use the box to open its original six-row storage screen. The screen supports normal inventory transfers and the original quick-move-all action.<br>
<br>
Filled Material Storage Boxes cannot be nested inside another Material Storage Box.
