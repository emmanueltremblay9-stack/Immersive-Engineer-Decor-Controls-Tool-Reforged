Sleeping Bag
Portable rest kit
<&recipe>Stand beside a lit vanilla campfire before using the Sleeping Bag at night in the Overworld. The fire must occupy one of the eight neighboring horizontal blocks at your feet's height; diagonals count. A fire directly under your feet, two blocks away, above or below you, unlit, or a soul campfire does not qualify.<br>
A successful rest advances time to morning without changing your spawn point. Nearby monsters prevent rest. Successful use applies a 400-tick cooldown and wears the bag outside Creative mode.<br>
