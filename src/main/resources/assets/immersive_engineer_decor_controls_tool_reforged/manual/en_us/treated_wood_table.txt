Treated Wood Table
Workshop furnishing
<&recipe>This block is a practical shop furnishing for work rooms and maintenance corners.<br>
<br>
Place this table directly north, south, east or west of a Treated Wood Stool at the same block height. Meals finished on that stool gain 30% of their normal saturation instead of its usual 20%. Several tables do not stack the bonus.<br>
<np>
Cake service
Ten percent while on foot
Place a real vanilla cake on this table. A player on foot receives 10% extra saturation only when actually eating a slice from that cake. Holding other food near the table gives no standing bonus. Candle cakes qualify when eaten; adding or extinguishing their candles does not.<br>
<br>
Seated players use the stool's 20% or 30% rule without stacking the standing bonus. Saturation cannot exceed hunger. Steel tables, crafting tables and other furniture do not qualify.<br>
