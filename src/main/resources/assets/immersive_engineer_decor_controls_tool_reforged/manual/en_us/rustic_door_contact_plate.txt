Rustic Door Pressure
Occupancy signal
<&recipe>This contact control supplies redstone while an entity occupies its detection area.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place it on the top face of a supporting block along the route to monitor. Entity contact activates it, and it stays on while the area remains occupied.<br>
<br>
Notes: It checks occupancy every 12 game ticks and switches off after the area clears.
