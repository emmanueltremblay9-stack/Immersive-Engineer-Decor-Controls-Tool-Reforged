Yellow LED
Signal feedback
<&recipe>This indicator shows whether the block it is attached to supplies a signal.<br>
<br>
Usage: Attach it to a solid face and power the supporting block to monitor doors, machines, alarms, or panels.<br>
<br>
Notes: It follows the supporting block's redstone or comparator output and refreshes once per second. Nearby power that does not reach that block will not activate it.
