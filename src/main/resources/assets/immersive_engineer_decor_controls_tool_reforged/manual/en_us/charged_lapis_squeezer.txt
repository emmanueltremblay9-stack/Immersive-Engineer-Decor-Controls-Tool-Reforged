Mind Squeezer
Experience condenser
<&recipe>The Mind Squeezer converts lapis and experience into Charged Lapis.<br>
<br>
Usage: Carry lapis lazuli and at least one experience level, then use the tool. Each conversion consumes one lapis and one level, costs 10% of maximum health, causes exhaustion and one second of blindness, and creates one Charged Lapis.<br>
<br>
The tool is reusable and does not wear down. It refuses use at 10% health or less, or without lapis or XP.
