Fluid Barrel
Fluid line hardware
<&recipe>The barrel stores up to 32,000 mB of water, lava or milk, one fluid type at a time. Use buckets for manual transfer. Connect fluid pipes above or below: horizontal faces do not expose a fluid connection.<br>
Open the screen to inspect the stored fluid and its amount. Empty the current fluid before changing types; the barrel is storage, not a fluid converter.<br>
