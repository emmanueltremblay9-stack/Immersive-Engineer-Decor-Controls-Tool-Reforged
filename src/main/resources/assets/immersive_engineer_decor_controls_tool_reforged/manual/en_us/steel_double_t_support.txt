Steel Double T Support
Structural frame part
<&recipe>This block supplies visible structure for machine halls and access frames.<br>
<br>
Usage: Rotate and place it as visible bracing for beams, poles, and platform edges.<br>
<br>
An Iron Bulb can mount flush beneath a free center. A mounted bulb and a steel pole connector cannot occupy the same center; placement of the conflicting part is rejected without spending its item. The bulb can be mined separately, or returned with the beam under normal tool and loot rules.<br>
