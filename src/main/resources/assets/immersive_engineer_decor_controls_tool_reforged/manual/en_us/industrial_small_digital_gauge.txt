Digital Gauge
Readable signal strength
<&recipe>This gauge displays the signal supplied by the block it is attached to.<br>
<br>
Usage: Mount it on the block whose signal you want to read. It follows that block's direct or indirect redstone power and comparator output.<br>
<br>
Notes: Displays a strength from 0 to 15. A source beside the gauge is not sufficient unless it powers the supporting block.
