Metal Crafting Table
Plate workstation
<&recipe>The Metal Crafting Table is a reinforced shop station with a dedicated tool slot, a full 3x3 crafting grid, and a clear output slot.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use the grid like a normal crafting table. To form IE plates, place an Engineer's Hammer in the tool slot and put the plate material in the grid, then take the formed plate from the output.<br>
<br>
The same dedicated tool slot performs two table-only operations. An Ore Crushing Hammer with an iron or gold ore-tag block produces two matching Immersive Engineering dusts. Immersive Engineering Engineer's Wire Cutters with an aluminum, copper, electrum, lead, or steel plate produce one matching wire. Ordinary shears do not work.<br>
<br>
These dust and wire operations do not work in the player crafting grid or at a vanilla Crafting Table. JEI lists them under Metal Crafting with this table as the workstation.<br>
<br>
Steel decorations use steel plates. Insert an Immersive Engineering hammer, hammer steel ingots into steel plates, then use plates to craft poles, catwalks, windows, doors, railings, and other steel decorations.<br>
