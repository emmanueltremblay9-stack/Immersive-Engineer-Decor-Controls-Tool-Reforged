Ore Crushing Hammer
Hand crushing tool
<&recipe>The Ore Crushing Hammer manually crushes aluminum, copper, gold, iron, lead, nickel, silver, and uranium ores into two matching Immersive Engineering dusts. Optional Mekanism support also adds osmium and tin.<br>
<br>
Usage: Combine one supported ore with the hammer in a normal crafting grid, or place the hammer in the Metal Crafting Table tool slot with one ore in its grid. The hammer takes durability damage and remains available until it breaks.<br>
<br>
It also wears down from mining and can knock targets back in close combat.
