Accessways
Walkways and supports
Steel poles, treated posts, grating, catwalks, railings, ladders, doors, windows, and hatches form compact service routes through factory interiors.<br>
<br>
Steel accessway parts use steel plates. Craft or place a Metal Crafting Table, insert an Immersive Engineering hammer, then hammer steel ingots into steel plates for poles, catwalks, windows, doors, railings, and other steel decorations.<br>
<br>
Place them where machines need service access, elevated paths, maintenance shafts, or safer edges.

Steel Double T Support
Structural frame part
<&steel_double_t_support>Use the Steel Double T Support item on a wall to place an upright wall support. Floor or ceiling clicks place the horizontal support, oriented by your horizontal facing. Wall supports connect to directly touching horizontal steel supports and ceiling treated-wood beams in either placement order.<br>
The older vertical-support item remains usable in saved inventories, but is hidden from the creative tab and normal JEI list. Picking or breaking its placed block returns the canonical Steel Double T Support item.<br>
An Iron Bulb can mount flush beneath a free center of a horizontal support. A mounted bulb and a steel pole connector cannot occupy that same center; conflicting placement is rejected without spending the item. The bulb can be mined separately, or returned with the beam under normal tool and loot rules.<br>
<br>
Double-T supports also accept one compatible Iron Bulb Light, Ceiling Edge Light, Inset Floor Edge Light or Inset Light on an internal structural face where the lamp fits without overlap. Aim at the actual metal mounting face; not every face or lamp orientation fits. You may place the support first or place it around an existing compatible lamp. A second attachment is rejected.<br>
Aim at the attached lamp to mine it separately, leaving the support. A lamp paid for in Survival can be returned once through normal tool and loot rules; Creative additions do not grant a paid refund. Mining the whole support returns its paid lamp with it under those same rules.<br>
<br>
The separate Treated Wood Beam is available in the creative catalogue without a crafting recipe. On a wall, click nearer a side edge for a horizontal beam or nearer the top/bottom for an upright beam; center and equal-distance clicks choose upright. Floor and ceiling clicks place a horizontal beam. A vertical treated-wood pole directly below a ceiling beam forms a matching connection without consuming the pole. This wood beam is not the steel support's lamp attachment system.<br><np>

Steel Catwalk
Service access fitting
<&steel_catwalk>The Steel Catwalk provides a walking deck with up to four independently fitted edge railings.<br>
<br>
Usage: Hold a Steel Railing and right-click near the edge to add or remove that side. Each addition costs one railing in Survival. Paid additions can be returned when removed; Creative additions do not create extra refunds.<br>
<br>
Aim at a railing and mine it to remove that side only. Mine the deck to remove the catwalk and its remaining paid railings. Normal tool and loot rules apply.
<np>
Steel Catwalk Stairs
Service access fitting
<&steel_catwalk_stairs>This block forms readable service routes around machinery.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place it as maintenance access around machines, upper floors, and service shafts.<br>
<br>
Hold Steel Railing and right-click the left or right side of the stairs to install or remove that side in place. Installing a side costs one railing in Survival; removing a purchased side returns it.<br>
Mine the aimed railing to remove only that side. Mine the step itself to remove the stairs and their remaining purchased railings.<br>
<br>
Click an open edge of a Steel Catwalk, Raised Steel Catwalk or Steel Floor Grating to attach the stair's high end; the flight descends away from the platform. A bottom-mounted catwalk places the stair one cell lower, while raised catwalks and grating place it at the same cell height. Click the low or high end of a stair to extend the flight one cell down or up. The selected edge or endpoint, not your look direction, chooses the connection.<br>
The separate Raised Steel Catwalk Stair uses the same placement and railing rules. With a matching wood, thin-steel or thick-steel pole or pole head below, it displays a matching V support. An upward-facing Treated Wood Pole Support also qualifies. The pole remains a separate block with its own collision and drops; the V is not an extra mined item. This variant currently has no crafting recipe and is available in the creative catalogue or through commands.<br>
<np>
Raised Steel Catwalk
Service access fitting
<&steel_catwalk_ta>The raised catwalk sits at the top of its block space. It connects down to thick or thin steel poles, straight treated-wood poles, and their heads. Changing or removing the pole updates the connection. Each connection includes the matching complete pole head, with its plate and braces beneath the deck. No separate pole-head item is needed.<br>
<br>
Click the top of a sturdy block to place the frame variant. Other placements use the plain deck unless a compatible pole is directly below.<br>
<br>
Holding another raised catwalk, click its top or underside to extend the route in the direction you face. The new piece stays at the same height.<br>
<br>
Click the underside of a plain deck with an Iron Inset Light to install it. Click again while holding an inset light to remove it. A light installed in survival consumes one item and returns it when removed or when the catwalk is mined. Older automatically selected light variants and creative additions do not refund a light item.<br>
<br>
You can also aim directly at the inset lamp from below and mine it normally to remove only the lamp. The deck, pole and railings stay in place. Survival refunds require a suitable harvesting tool; creative and unpaid lamps give no item. Aim at the deck itself to mine the whole catwalk.<br>
<np>
Steel Railing
Service access fitting
<&steel_railing>A Steel Railing can form up to four separate edge sides within one block space.<br>
<br>
Usage: The clicked edge chooses the first side. Hold another Steel Railing and right-click the desired edge to add or remove that side. Survival additions consume one railing and paid additions can be returned on removal.<br>
<br>
Mine the aimed side to remove only it. Removing the final side clears the railing block. Creative additions do not create extra refunds; normal tool and loot rules apply.

Steel Mesh Fence Gate
Service access fitting
<&steel_mesh_fence_gate>This manually operated gate can be one or two blocks tall.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place a second segment directly above the first to make a tall gate. Right-click either segment to open or close both. Breaking either segment removes the whole tall gate.<br>
<br>
Two matching gates beside each other, aligned in the same plane and of equal height, can open together with an empty-main-hand click. A row of three is not a linked pair. These gates remain manually controlled, without redstone activation.

Metal Rung Ladder
Service access fitting
<&metal_rung_ladder>This wall-mounted ladder provides vertical service access.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place it against a supporting wall. To climb through an Iron Hatch above, put the ladder directly below the hatch and align the ladder's support edge with the hatch's open leaf.<br>
<br>
The hatch must be open. A closed or misaligned hatch does not continue the climb.

Metal Sliding Door
Industrial access point
<&metal_sliding_door>The Metal Sliding Door opens manually or with redstone and can form a matching pair.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place two doors side by side at the same height and facing the same direction. Right-click either door, at either height, to open or close the pair. A third adjacent door is not added to that pair.<br>
<br>
Power reaching either paired door holds both open. They close when the power is removed; clicking cannot close a powered pair.
<np>
Iron Hatch
Industrial access point
<&iron_hatch>The Iron Hatch opens by right-click or redstone. Redstone keeps it open until the signal is removed.<br>
<br>
Usage: For a climbable exit, place a ladder directly below and align its supporting edge with the open hatch's leaf. Vanilla ladders, Metal Rung Ladders, and Staggered Metal Steps can continue through this aligned opening.<br>
<br>
A closed hatch or a ladder on a different side does not provide this climbing connection.
