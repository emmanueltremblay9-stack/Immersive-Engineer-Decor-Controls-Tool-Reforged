Tracker
Stored position marker
<&recipe>Click a block or living entity with the Tracker to store it as the target. Its 3D pointer follows tracked entities as they move and points toward targets in the current dimension.<br>
<br>
Usage: To clear an existing target, look almost straight down and click a block or entity. The tooltip shows the target and its dimension.<br>
<br>
Use it to mark machines, tunnel branches, remote controls, or unfinished construction sites before returning with tools and materials.
