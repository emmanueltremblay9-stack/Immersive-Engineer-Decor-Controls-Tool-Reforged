Industrial Construction
Factory walls and trim
General construction fittings cover doors, windows, lights, warning signs, trim, and structural parts that do not belong to a more specific section.<br>
<br>
Use these pages for factory rooms, service corridors, hazard marking, and practical industrial detail work.
<np>
Iron Bulb Light
Compact work light
<&iron_bulb_light>This block provides compact industrial lighting for corridors, catwalks, and work bays.<br>
<br>
Usage: Mount it on ceilings, walls, or edges where the room needs clear work lighting.<br>
<br>
Steel Double T mounting: use the Iron Bulb on the beam's underside to mount it centered against the metal. The center must be free of a steel pole connector. Aim at the bulb and mine with a suitable pickaxe to dismantle it without breaking the beam; release attack before mining the beam. Purchased bulbs are returned once through normal tool, loot, and gamerule handling when dismantled or when their beam is mined.<br>

Inset Light
Compact work light
<&iron_inset_light>This block provides compact industrial lighting for corridors, catwalks, and work bays.<br>
<br>
Usage: Mount it on ceilings, walls, or edges where the room needs clear work lighting.<br>
<br>
To light a plain Raised Steel Catwalk, use an Inset Light on its underside. Installing it costs one light in Survival. Use another Inset Light on the underside to remove the installed light; a paid light is returned outside Creative mode. Supported or framed variants do not accept a new light through this gesture.<br>
You can instead aim directly at the inset lamp from below and mine it with a suitable tool, leaving the deck in place. Aim away from the lamp to mine the deck. Normal permissions and loot rules apply, and legacy or Creative-installed lights do not create paid refunds. Adding a pole after installation preserves the lamp; removing it reveals the current pole support again.<br>
Inset Lights can also attach to a compatible internal face of a Double-T support. See Steel Double T Support for fitting and separate-removal rules.<br>
