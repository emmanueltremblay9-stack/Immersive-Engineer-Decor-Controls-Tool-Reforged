Dimmer
Variable signal
<&recipe>The Dimmer selects redstone strength from the point clicked on its panel.<br>
<br>
Usage: Right-click higher or lower on a wall-mounted panel to select stronger or weaker output. On a floor or ceiling, the north-south click position sets the strength instead.<br>
<br>
Notes: Each click selects the output directly; repeated clicks do not cycle through the values.
