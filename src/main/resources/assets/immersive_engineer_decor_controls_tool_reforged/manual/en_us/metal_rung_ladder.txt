Metal Rung Ladder
Service access fitting
<&recipe>This wall-mounted ladder provides vertical service access.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place it against a supporting wall. To climb through an Iron Hatch above, put the ladder directly below the hatch and align the ladder's support edge with the hatch's open leaf.<br>
<br>
The hatch must be open. A closed or misaligned hatch does not continue the climb.
