Steel Mesh Fence Gate
Service access fitting
<&recipe>This manually operated gate can be one or two blocks tall.<br>
<br>
Usage: Place a second segment directly above the first to make a tall gate. Right-click either segment to open or close both. Breaking either segment removes the whole tall gate.<br>
<br>
Two matching gates beside each other, aligned in the same plane and of equal height, can open together with an empty-main-hand click. A row of three is not a linked pair. These gates remain manually controlled, without redstone activation.
