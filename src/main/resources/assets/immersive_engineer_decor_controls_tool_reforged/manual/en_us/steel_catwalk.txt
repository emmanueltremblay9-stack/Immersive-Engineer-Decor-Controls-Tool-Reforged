Steel Catwalk
Service access fitting
<&recipe>The Steel Catwalk provides a walking deck with up to four independently fitted edge railings.<br>
<br>
Usage: Hold a Steel Railing and right-click near the edge to add or remove that side. Each addition costs one railing in Survival. Paid additions can be returned when removed; Creative additions do not create extra refunds.<br>
<br>
Aim at a railing and mine it to remove that side only. Mine the deck to remove the catwalk and its remaining paid railings. Normal tool and loot rules apply.
