Diving Air Capsule
Emergency air reserve
<&recipe>The Diving Air Capsule activates automatically when air falls to 30 percent or less and restores 70 percent of maximum air.<br>
<br>
Usage: Carry it in inventory or a supported Curios slot. It contains ten uses and cannot be activated manually.<br>
<br>
Carry it for flooded mines, submerged pipe runs, and pump-room repairs.
