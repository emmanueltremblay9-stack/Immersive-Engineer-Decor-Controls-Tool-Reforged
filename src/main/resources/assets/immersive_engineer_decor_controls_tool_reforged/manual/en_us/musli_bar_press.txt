Muslee Bar Press
Food press
<&recipe>The reusable Muslee Bar Press stores up to 512 accepted seeds and 768 hunger points from ordinary food.<br>
<br>
Usage: Open its original input/output screen and insert wheat, melon, pumpkin, or beetroot seeds plus safe edible food. Each bar costs one seed and eight hunger points; unused points remain stored in the press.<br>
<br>
The press is reusable and does not consume durability.
