Engineer's Tools
Portable workshop gear
The Engineer's Tools set adds handheld equipment, mine markers, emergency consumables, field food, and small material-processing aids to the same manual that covers the decor and control blocks.<br>
<br>
These tools are meant for work away from a fully equipped shop: mark the route, carry supplies, recover from mistakes, and finish small processing jobs on site.
<np>
REDIA Tool
Diamond-covered utility
<&redia_tool>The REDIA Tool is a durable field utility for quick ground work. Use it on workable soil to till farmland.<br>
<br>
Use it against a stable face while carrying torches to place a torch marker from your inventory without wearing REDIA. Ground work, shearing, axe work, and tree felling consume durability.
<np>
Ore Crushing Hammer
Hand crushing tool
<&crushing_hammer>The Ore Crushing Hammer crushes aluminum, copper, gold, iron, lead, nickel, silver, and uranium ore into two Immersive Engineering dusts. Optional Mekanism support also restores osmium and tin crushing.<br>
<br>
Use the hammer and ore together in a normal crafting grid, or put the hammer in the Metal Crafting Table tool slot and one ore in the grid.<br>
<br>
The hammer takes durability damage and remains in the table's tool slot until it breaks. It also wears from hard mining and can shove close threats away.
<np>
Tracker
Stored position marker
<&tracker>Click a block or living entity with the Tracker to store it as the target. The 3D pointer follows tracked entities as they move and points toward targets in the current dimension.<br>
<br>
To clear an existing target, look almost straight down and click a block or entity. The tooltip reports the target and dimension.
<np>
Ariadne Coal
Mine route marker
<&ariadne_coal>Ariadne Coal places quick black arrow route markers on stable mine faces. Use it on a solid face to mark the next turn, exit, or branch point; the marker supports eight clicked-face directions, including diagonals.<br>
<br>
Ariadne Coal is a reusable 100-use marker tool. Each successful marker placement costs one durability. It refuses unsupported faces instead of wearing the item.
<np>
Auto Stim Pack
Emergency injector
<&stimpack>The Auto Stim Pack begins a 20-tick injection sequence automatically when health falls to three hearts or less. It then heals three hearts and briefly grants movement speed, regeneration, resistance, and fire resistance.<br>
<br>
It works from inventory or a supported Curios slot and contains two injections. It cannot be activated manually.
<np>
Sleeping Bag
Portable rest kit
<&sleeping_bag>Use the Sleeping Bag on the top of a non-bed block to enter Minecraft's normal sleep process without setting a spawn point.<br>
<br>
Normal time, dimension, monster-safety, and multiplayer sleeping rules still apply. The bag is reusable and does not consume durability.
<np>
Material Storage Box
Construction supply box
<&material_box>The Material Storage Box is a portable 54-slot inventory for construction runs. It preserves arbitrary item stacks and their components.<br>
<br>
Use the box to open its original six-row storage screen. Filled boxes cannot be nested inside other Material Boxes.
<np>
Diving Air Capsule
Emergency air reserve
<&diving_capsule>The Diving Air Capsule activates automatically when air falls to 30 percent or less and restores 70 percent of maximum air.<br>
<br>
It works from inventory or a supported Curios slot, contains ten uses, and cannot be activated manually.
<np>
Muslee Bar Press
Food press
<&musli_bar_press>The reusable Muslee Bar Press stores up to 512 accepted seeds and 768 hunger points from ordinary food.<br>
<br>
Open its original input/output screen and insert wheat, melon, pumpkin, or beetroot seeds plus safe edible food. Each bar costs one seed and eight hunger points; unused points remain stored in the press.
<np>
Muslee Melange Power Bar
Fast shop snack
The Muslee Melange Power Bar is a fast, always-edible six-point food made in the portable press.<br>
<br>
Use it as compact field food while installing long cable runs, pipes, and factory fixtures.
<np>
Mind Squeezer
Experience condenser
<&charged_lapis_squeezer>The Mind Squeezer converts one lapis lazuli and one experience level from your inventory into Charged Lapis.<br>
<br>
The process hurts, exhausts, and briefly blinds the user on every successful conversion. It refuses to run without lapis, without XP, or when the user is too close to death.
<np>
Charged Lapis
Stored experience
<&charged_lapis>Charged Lapis stores one experience level. Consume it to restore the level, clear active effects, and recover a small amount of health.<br>
<br>
Craft a Mind Squeezer first; Charged Lapis is produced by using that tool with lapis and XP available.
