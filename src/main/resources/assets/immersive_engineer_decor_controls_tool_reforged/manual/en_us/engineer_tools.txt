Engineer's Tools
Portable workshop gear
The Engineer's Tools set adds handheld equipment, mine markers, emergency consumables, field food, and small material-processing aids to the same manual that covers the decor and control blocks.<br>
<br>
These tools are meant for work away from a fully equipped shop: mark the route, carry supplies, recover from mistakes, and finish small processing jobs on site.
<np>
REDIA Tool
Diamond-covered utility
<&redia_tool>The REDIA Tool is a durable diamond-grade multi-tool for field work around temporary sites, workshops, and mine entrances.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use it while carrying torches to place one torch from your inventory without wearing REDIA. Sneak-use it to shear plants or cycle ground between dirt, farmland, coarse dirt, and dirt path. The original axe fallback applies from the bottom face. Sneak while breaking logs to fell connected tree blocks.<br>
<br>
Repair it with diamonds. Over-repairing a fully repaired REDIA Tool restores the original Efficiency and Fortune progression.
<np>
Ore Crushing Hammer
Hand crushing tool
<&crushing_hammer>The Ore Crushing Hammer is a manual processing tool for early iron and gold work.<br>
<br>
Usage: Craft it with raw iron, raw gold, iron ore, deepslate iron ore, gold ore, or deepslate gold ore to produce Immersive Engineering iron or gold dust. The hammer takes durability damage and returns to the crafting grid until it breaks.<br>
<br>
It also wears down from mining and can knock targets back in close combat.
<np>
Tracker
Stored position marker
<&tracker>The Tracker stores a block position directly in the item.<br>
<br>
Usage: Click a block to save its dimension and coordinates. The tooltip shows the stored position. Sneak-use the Tracker to clear the target.<br>
<br>
Use it to mark machines, tunnel branches, remote controls, or unfinished construction sites before returning with tools and materials.
<np>
Ariadne Coal
Mine route marker
<&ariadne_coal>Ariadne Coal is a quick route marker for shafts, branch mines, and temporary underground work.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use it on a stable block face to place a black arrow marker on that border. The marker reads the clicked point on the face and supports eight directions, including diagonals, helping mark turns, exits, dead ends, and supply routes without carrying separate signposts.<br>
<br>
Ariadne Coal is a reusable 100-use marker tool. Each successful marker placement costs one durability, and unsupported faces are rejected without wearing the item.
<np>
Auto Stim Pack
Emergency injector
<&stimpack>The Auto Stim Pack is an emergency recovery item for dangerous maintenance work.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use it manually to heal, gain brief protection, and get a short movement boost. When carried in inventory, it can also trigger automatically at low health, then enters cooldown.<br>
<br>
Keep one available before testing high places, exposed wiring, or hostile mine routes.
<np>
Sleeping Bag
Portable rest kit
<&sleeping_bag>Stand beside a lit vanilla campfire before using the Sleeping Bag at night in the Overworld. The fire must occupy one of the eight neighboring horizontal blocks at your feet's height; diagonals count. A fire directly under your feet, two blocks away, above or below you, unlit, or a soul campfire does not qualify.<br>
A successful rest advances time to morning without changing your spawn point. Nearby monsters prevent rest. Successful use applies a 400-tick cooldown and wears the bag outside Creative mode.<br><np>
Material Storage Box
Construction supply box
<&material_box>The Material Storage Box carries a large reserve of one plain, unmodified stackable construction material.<br>
<br>
Usage: Hold the box in the main hand and a material stack in the offhand to load that material into the box. Use the box with an empty offhand to pull one stack back out.<br>
<br>
The box holds up to 512 items, keeps only one material type at a time, rejects nested boxes, non-stackable gear, and modified named or data-bearing stacks, and reports its stored contents in the tooltip.
<np>
Diving Air Capsule
Emergency air reserve
<&diving_capsule>The Diving Air Capsule is an emergency air item for underwater work.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use it manually to restore air. When carried in inventory, it can also trigger automatically when air is nearly gone, then enters cooldown.<br>
<br>
Carry it for flooded mines, submerged pipe runs, and pump-room repairs.
<np>
Muslee Bar Press
Food press
<&musli_bar_press>The Muslee Bar Press turns common food supplies into compact field rations.<br>
<br>
Usage: Carry bread, an apple, and wheat, melon, pumpkin, beetroot, or Industrial Hemp seeds, then use the press. A successful press consumes one of each ingredient, checks that the output can fit, creates four Muslee Melange Power Bars, and damages the press.<br>
<br>
If an ingredient is missing or the inventory has no room for the bars, nothing is consumed.
<np>
Muslee Melange Power Bar
Fast shop snack
<&musli_bar>The Muslee Melange Power Bar is a compact food item made from bread, seeds, and fruit.<br>
<br>
Usage: Eat it as field food while placing controls, pipes, platforms, or long machine-room runs.
<np>
Mind Squeezer
Experience condenser
<&charged_lapis_squeezer>The Mind Squeezer converts lapis and experience into Charged Lapis.<br>
<br>
Usage: Carry lapis lazuli and at least one experience level, then use the tool. A successful squeeze consumes one level and one lapis, hurts the user, damages the squeezer, and creates one Charged Lapis.<br>
<br>
It refuses to run when the user lacks lapis, lacks XP, or is too close to death.
<np>
Charged Lapis
Stored experience
<&charged_lapis>Charged Lapis stores one experience level created through the Mind Squeezer.<br>
<br>
Usage: Consume it to restore one level, clear active effects, and recover a small amount of health.<br>
<br>
The recipe widget shows the Mind Squeezer because Charged Lapis is produced by using that tool with lapis and XP available.
<np>
Coarse Iron Grit
Legacy crushed iron feed
<&iron_grit>Coarse Iron Grit is retained for existing worlds that already contain the old hammer output.<br>
<br>
Usage: Smelt or blast it into iron ingots. New Ore Crushing Hammer recipes now produce Immersive Engineering iron dust instead.

<br>
Usage: Smelt or blast it into gold ingots. New Ore Crushing Hammer recipes now produce Immersive Engineering gold dust instead.
