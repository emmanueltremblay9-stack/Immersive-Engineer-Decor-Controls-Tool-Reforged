Labeled Crate
54-slot storage with a content hint
<&recipe>This storage crate restores the original 9 by 6 inventory and front-mounted content hint. The hint slot holds one item and is hidden from hoppers and other automation.<br>
<br>
Usage: Right-click the crate to open storage. Shift-right-click the front plate to edit any preserved stock label. Shift-click moves items; Ctrl+Shift-click moves matching item stacks with the same components.<br>
<br>
Recipe: 4 Immersive Engineering Steel Fences and 4 Old Industrial Wood Planks.<br>
