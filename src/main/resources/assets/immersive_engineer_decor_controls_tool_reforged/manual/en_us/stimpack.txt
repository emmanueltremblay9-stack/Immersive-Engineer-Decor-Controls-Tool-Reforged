Auto Stim Pack
Emergency injector
<&recipe>The Auto Stim Pack begins a 20-tick injection sequence automatically when health falls to three hearts or less. It then heals three hearts and briefly grants movement speed, regeneration, resistance, and fire resistance.<br>
<br>
Usage: Carry it in inventory or a supported Curios slot. It contains two injections and cannot be activated manually.<br>
<br>
Keep one available before testing high places, exposed wiring, or hostile mine routes.
