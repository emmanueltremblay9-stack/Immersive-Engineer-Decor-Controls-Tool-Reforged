REDIA multi-tool
Utility clad in diamond

<br>
Orders: Carry torches and use her to place one from yer loot bag without wearin' REDIA. Creep while usin' her to shear plants or cycle ground through filth, fertile land, rough filth and trodden path. Her original hatchet fallback works from the bottom face. Creep while breakin' logs to fell connected tree blocks.<br>
<br>
