Metal craftin' table
Workstation fer plates
<&metal_crafting_table>The metal craftin' table be a reinforced workshop station with a hammer slot, a 3x3 craftin' grid and a clear output slot.<br>
<br>
Orders: Use the grid fer ordinary craftin'. Fer IE plates, berth an Engineer's Hammer in the tool slot and put the plate material in the grid, then take the result. Steel finery calls fer steel plates to make poles, gangways, portholes, doors, guardrails and other steel pieces.<br>
