Gangways and passages
Gangways and braces
Steel poles, treated posts, grating, service gangways, guardrails, riggin', doors, portholes and hatches make compact service routes through yer factory.<br>
<br>
Steel passage fittings call fer steel plates. Craft or place a metal craftin' table, put an Immersive Engineering hammer aboard, then hammer steel ingots into plates fer poles, gangways, portholes, doors, guardrails and other steel finery.<br>
<br>
Set them where contraptions need service access, raised paths, maintenance shafts or safer edges.
