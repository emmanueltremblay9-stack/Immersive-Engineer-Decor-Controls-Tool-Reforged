Ariadne's coal
Marks fer mine routes
<&recipe>Ariadne's coal marks routes swiftly through shafts, branch mines and temporary work below ground.<br>
<br>
Orders: Use her on a firm block face to put a black arrow on that border. Where ye click decides its direction, with eight choices includin' diagonals. Mark turns, ways out, dead ends and supply routes without haulin' separate signposts.<br>
<br>
Ariadne's coal be a reusable markin' tool with 100 uses. Each mark ye set successfully costs one durability. Faces she cannot mark are refused without wear.
