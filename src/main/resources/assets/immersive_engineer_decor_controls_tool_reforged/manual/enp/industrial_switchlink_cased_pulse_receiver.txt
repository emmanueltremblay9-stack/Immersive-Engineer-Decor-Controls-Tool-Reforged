Cased Beat Catcher
Linked switchgear
<&recipe>A cased linked beat catcher gives faraway switchboards a short redstone starter.<br>
<br>
Use: Click it with an Ender Mergroat to make a linked mergroat. Right-click it or start the linked mergroat to send a short beat.<br>
<br>
Mind: The beat resets itself after its set length.
