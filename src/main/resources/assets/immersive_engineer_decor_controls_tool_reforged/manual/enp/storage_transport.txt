Keeping and Carrying
Inholding and flow fittings
Keeping and carrying fittings handle crates, barrels, funnels and flowgates for close-fitting workstead paths.<br>
<br>
Use these pages when laying out stockrooms, pipe runs, upkeep vats and ruled flow lines.
<np>
Named Crate
Changeable stock name
<&labeled_crate>This named crate makes stockroom sorting clear. Its fore sheet can be written on like a small workshop markboard.<br>
<br>
Use: Right-click the name sheet to change the crate name. Right-click the rest of the crate to open its stockholding for fittings, bits and upkeep supplies.<br>
<np>
Flowstuff Barrel
Flow-line gear
<&fluid_barrel>This small flow-line fitting suits little vats, pipe runs, gathering spots and redstone-ruled flow.<br>
<br>
Use: Set it where pipes or buckets can reach the vat side.<br>
<np>
Flowstuff Funnel
Flow-line gear
<&small_fluid_funnel>The Small Flowstuff Funnel is a small flow-line fitting for little vats, pipe runs, gathering spots and redstone-ruled flow.<br>
<br>
Use: Put it beneath the flowstuff gathering spot and send what it holds into vats or lower pipes.<br>
