Diving Air Capsule
Emergency air reserve
<&recipe>Heed this: The Diving Air Capsule is an emergency air thing for underwater work.<br>
<br>
Usage: Use it manually to restore air. When carried in inholding, it can also trigger by itself when air is nearly gone, then enters cooldown.<br>
<br>
Carry it for flooded mines, submerged pipe runs, and pump-room repairs.
