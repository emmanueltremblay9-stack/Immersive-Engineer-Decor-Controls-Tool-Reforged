Bemeter Switch
Backing token
<&recipe>A small watcher switch reads its backing clot and turns that reading into a plain board outflow.<br>
<br>
Use: Fix it to inholdings, vats or redstone gear. When the backing clot gives a Bemeter, holder or redstone outflow, the switch sends full-strength redstone.<br>
<br>
Mind: Turn stock levels or hidden wiring into clear on/off switch lines.
