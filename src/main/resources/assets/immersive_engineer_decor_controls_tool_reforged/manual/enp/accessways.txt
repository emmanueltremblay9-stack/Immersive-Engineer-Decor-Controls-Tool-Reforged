Walkways
Walkways and Braces
Steel poles, treated posts, grates, walkways, guardrails, ladders, doors, windows and hatches make close-fitting upkeep paths through workstead halls.<br>
<br>
Steel walkway fittings use steel sheets. Craft or set a Bloom Workbench, put in an Immersive Engineering hammer, then hammer steel ingots into sheets for poles, walkways, windows, doors, guardrails and other steel decking.<br>
<br>
Set them where looms need upkeep paths, raised walkways, upkeep shafts or guarded edges.
