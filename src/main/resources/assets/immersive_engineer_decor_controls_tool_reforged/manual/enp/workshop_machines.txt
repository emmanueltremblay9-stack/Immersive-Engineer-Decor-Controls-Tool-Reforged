Bloom Workbench
Sheet workstead
<&metal_crafting_table>The Bloom Workbench is a strengthened workshop stand with a hammer slot, a 3x3 crafting grid and a clear outward slot.<br>
<br>
Use: Use the grid for everyday crafting. For IE sheets, put an Engineer's Hammer in the tool slot and the sheet stuff in the grid before taking the yield. Steel decking uses steel sheets for poles, walkways, windows, doors, guardrails and other steel fittings.<br>
