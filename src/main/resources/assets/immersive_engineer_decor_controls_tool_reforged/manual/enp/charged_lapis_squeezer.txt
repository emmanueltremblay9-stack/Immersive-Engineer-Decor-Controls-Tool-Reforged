Mind Squeezer
XP squeeze loom
<&recipe>The Mind Squeezer turns Hewnstone and XP into Loaded Hewnstone.<br>
<br>
Use: Carry Hewnstone and at least one XP level, then use the tool. A good squeeze spends one level and one Hewnstone, hurts you, wears the squeezer and makes one Loaded Hewnstone.<br>
<br>
It will not work when you lack Hewnstone or XP, or are too near death.
