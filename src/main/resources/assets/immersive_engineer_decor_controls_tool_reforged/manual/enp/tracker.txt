Tracker
Stored stead marker
<&recipe>Heed this: The Tracker stores a block stead directly in the thing.<br>
<br>
Usage: Click a block to save its world-room and bearings. The helptext shows the stored stead. Sneak-use the Tracker to clear the target.<br>
<br>
Use it to mark machines, tunnel branches, remote controls, or unfinished building sites before returning with tools and stuffs.
