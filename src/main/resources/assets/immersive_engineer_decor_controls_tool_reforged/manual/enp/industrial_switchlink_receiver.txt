Switch Link Catcher
Linked switchgear
<&recipe>A linked catcher gives faraway switchboards a full-strength redstone mark.<br>
<br>
Use: Click it with an Ender Mergroat to make a linked mergroat. Right-click the catcher itself to turn it by hand.<br>
<br>
Mind: Sneak-using a linked mergroat turns this catcher on or off.
