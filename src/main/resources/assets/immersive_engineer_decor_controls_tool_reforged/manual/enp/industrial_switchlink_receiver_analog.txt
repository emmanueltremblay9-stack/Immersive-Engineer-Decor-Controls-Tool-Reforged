Graded Link Catcher
Linked switchgear
<&recipe>A linked catcher gives switchboards a seen mark for faraway use.<br>
<br>
Use: Click it with an Ender Mergroat to make a linked mergroat. Sneak-use that mergroat to start the catcher from afar.<br>
<br>
Mind: This catcher sends a full redstone token when started.
