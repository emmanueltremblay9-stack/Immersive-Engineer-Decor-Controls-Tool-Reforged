Loaded Hewnstone
XP held in store
<&recipe>Loaded Hewnstone holds one XP level, made with the Mind Squeezer.<br>
<br>
Use: Eat it to get one level back, clear ongoing workings and regain a little health.<br>
<br>
The crafting viewer shows the Mind Squeezer because Loaded Hewnstone is made by using that tool while Hewnstone and XP are at hand.
