Clinker and Stonework
Tile and stonelime shells
Clinker, slag tiles, strengthened stonelime and shieldglass give weighty outer layers to hot rooms, workshops and guarded sightways.<br>
<br>
Finish the edges with matching halfclots, stairs, slivers and walls while keeping the same workstead building style.
