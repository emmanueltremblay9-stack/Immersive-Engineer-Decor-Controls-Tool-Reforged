Workshop Engines
Engines and workshop inlets/outlets
Workshop engines afford compact crafts, item movement, smelting, disposal, gathering and other useful labours within a single block.<br>
<br>
Open the following pages for each engine’s recipe, picture, lore and counsel for use.

Metal Crafts Table
Workstation for Plates
<&metal_crafting_table>The Metal Crafts Table is a strengthened workshop station with a hammer slot, a 3x3 crafts grid and a clear outlet slot.<br>
<br>
Use: Work ordinary crafts in the grid. For IE plates, put an Engineer’s Hammer in the tool slot and the plate material in the grid, then take thy result. Steel adornments require steel plates for poles, walkways, windows, doors, railings and other steel fittings.<br>
<np>
Manufactory Conveyer
Convenience for the Workshop Floor
<&factory_hopper>This block moves stacks of items through a close-fitting manufactory case.<br>
<br>
Use: Face it towards the inventory line, then set reach, stack count, timing and redstone mode in its window.<br>
<np>
Manufactory Dropper
Convenience for the Workshop Floor
<&factory_dropper>This block casts forth stored items from the face thou aimst.<br>
<br>
Use: Aim its front at the place of release, fill its inward slots, and set force, angle, delay, filter logic and redstone mode in its window.<br>
<np>
