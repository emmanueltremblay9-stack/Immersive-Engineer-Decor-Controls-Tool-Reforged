Redstone Metre Switch
Signal from the Attached Block
<&recipe>A compact sensor switch reads the block that bears it, turning that reading into a simple panel output.<br>
<br>
Use: Mount it upon inventories, tanks or redstone fittings. When the attached block reports comparator, vessel or redstone output, the switch sends redstone at full strength.<br>
<br>
Note: Use it to turn storage levels or hidden wiring into clear lines of on/off control.
