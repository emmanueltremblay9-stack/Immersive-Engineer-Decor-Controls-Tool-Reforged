Storage & Carriage
Fittings for Inventory and Flow
Storage and carriage fittings provide crates, barrels, funnels and valves for compact routing through the manufactory.<br>
<br>
Consult these pages when planning stockrooms, pipe runs, service tanks and governed liquid lines.
<np>
Labelled Crate
Stock Label to Alter
<&labeled_crate>This labelled crate serves clear sorting in thy stockroom. Its front plate may be altered as a little workshop sign.<br>
<br>
Use: Right-click the label plate to alter the crate’s label. Right-click the rest of the crate to open storage for fittings, parts and supplies for upkeep.<br>
<np>
Barrel for Liquids
Fittings for Liquid Lines
<&fluid_barrel>This compact fitting serves liquid lines for little tanks, pipe runs, gathering points and flow governed by redstone.<br>
<br>
Use: Set it where pipes or buckets may reach the tank face.<br>
<np>
Funnel for Liquids
Fittings for Liquid Lines
<&small_fluid_funnel>The Little Funnel for Gathering Liquids is a compact fitting for little tanks, pipe runs, gathering points and flow governed by redstone.<br>
<br>
Use: Put it beneath the point of liquid gathering, and conduct its store into tanks or pipes below.<br>
