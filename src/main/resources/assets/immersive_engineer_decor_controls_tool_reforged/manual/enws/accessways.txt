Passageways
Walkways and Supports
Steel poles, treated posts, grating, walkways, railings, ladders, doors, windows and hatches make compact paths of service through the manufactory.<br>
<br>
Steel passageway fittings require steel plates. Craft or set a Metal Crafts Table, put in an Immersive Engineering hammer, then hammer steel ingots into plates for poles, walkways, windows, doors, railings and other steel adornments.<br>
<br>
Set them where engines require paths for service, raised ways, shafts for upkeep or better-guarded edges.
