Governance by Redstone
Gauges and Fittings for Signals
Control fittings make hidden redstone plain to read through manufactory panels, watchers, indicators, switches, alarms and linked signal fittings.<br>
<br>
Consult these pages when building operator stations, signal boards, safety circuits and controls for use from afar.

<&industrial_switchlink_pulse_relay>A fitting in the fashion of a relay affords thy panel a compact local switching point.<br>
<br>
Use: Right-click to turn a full-strength output on or off for nearby circuits. Choose receiver blocks when thou requirest activation by linked pearl.<br>
<br>
Note: Whilst turned on, it sends redstone at full strength.
<np>
Switch Link Receiver
Control through a Link
<&industrial_switchlink_receiver>A linked receiver affords distant panels a redstone target of full strength.<br>
<br>
Use: Click it with an Ender Pearl to fashion a linked pearl. Right-click the receiver itself to turn it on or off by hand.<br>
<br>
Note: Sneak-use a linked pearl to turn this receiver on or off.
<np>

Switch Link Pearl
Control through a Link
<&switchlink_pearl>A linked pearl remembers a receiver target for distant control of the manufactory.<br>
<br>
Use: Click a Switch Link receiver with an Ender Pearl to fashion one. Sneak-use the linked pearl to set off its remembered receiver.<br>
<br>
Note: The target must be loaded, within reach and in thy own dimension.
