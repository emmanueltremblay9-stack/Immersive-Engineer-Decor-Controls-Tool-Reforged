ilo REDIA
ilo pona pi selo kiwen laso
<&recipe>ilo REDIA li ilo pali mute pi mute pona suli pi wawa kiwen laso tawa pali ma lon poka pi ma pali lili tenpo, pi tomo pali, pi lupa kama kiwen ma.<br>
<br>
kepeken: o kepeken e ona lon jo pi palisa suno tawa pana e palisa suno wan tan jo sina kepeken lili ala pi mute pona REDIA. o tawa anpa li kepeken e ona tawa kipisi kasi anu tawa ante sike e ma lon ma, ma mama, ma pi kiwen lili, en ma nasin. pali open pi ilo kipisi li kama tan sinpin anpa. o tawa anpa lon pakala sijelo kasi tawa kipisi e leko kasi wan.<br>
<br>
o pona e ona kepeken kiwen laso. sina pona e ilo REDIA pi pona ale tawa mute pi suli tawa mute pona pi ilo sin la ni li pana sin e nasin kama open pi wawa pakala en mani mute.
