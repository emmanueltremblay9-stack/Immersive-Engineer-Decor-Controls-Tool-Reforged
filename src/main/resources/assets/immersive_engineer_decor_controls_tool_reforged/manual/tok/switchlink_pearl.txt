<&recipe>sike wan li awen e ma kama pi ilo kama jo tawa ilo lawa weka pi tomo pali.<br>
<br>
kepeken: o luka e ilo kama jo pi wan ilo lawa kepeken sike pi ma End tawa pali e ona. o tawa anpa li kepeken sike wan tawa open e ilo kama jo awen.<br>
<br>
sona: ma kama li wile lon sona musi, li wile lon ma sama pi nasin ale, li wile lon suli nasin ken.
