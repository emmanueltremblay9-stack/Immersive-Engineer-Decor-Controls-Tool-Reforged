ilo lawa sama
wawa wan
<&recipe>ilo lawa sona lili li lukin e leko pi wan ona li ante e sona ni tawa pana supa pona lili.<br>
<br>
kepeken: o wan e ona tawa poki jo, tawa poki telo, anu tawa ilo Redstone. leko wan li toki e pana pi ilo ante wawa, pi poki, anu pi Redstone la ilo lawa li pana e wawa Redstone suli nanpa wan.<br>
<br>
sona: o kepeken e ona tawa ante e mute jo anu linja pi lukin ala tawa linja lawa open/pini pi pona lukin.
