Jaranjarada alwaaxda duugga ah
Alwaaxda goobta shaqada ee duugga ah
<&recipe>Jaranjarada alwaaxda warshadeed ee duugga ahi waxay ku dartaa alwaax duug ah oo goob shaqo, loogu talagalay sagxado, tallaabooyin, albaabbo iyo qurxin gudaha ah oo diirran.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: U adeegso sagxadaha goobaha shaqada ee duugga ah, jaranjarooyin, albaabbo iyo qurxinta geesaha.<br>
