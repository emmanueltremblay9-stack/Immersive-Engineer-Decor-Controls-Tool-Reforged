Saxanka klinkarka taagan
Dhismaha lebenka ee danbasku madoobeeyey
<&recipe>Saxanka klinkarka taagan ee qaabaysan wuxuu hoolalka foornooyinka, saldhigyada qiiqmareennada iyo darbiyada adeegga ku daraa dhisme leben oo danbasku madoobeeyey.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: U adeegso darbi, qurxin geeseed ama daboolka aagga kulaylka ee ku wareegsan foornooyinka iyo kuleyliyeyaasha biyaha.<br>
