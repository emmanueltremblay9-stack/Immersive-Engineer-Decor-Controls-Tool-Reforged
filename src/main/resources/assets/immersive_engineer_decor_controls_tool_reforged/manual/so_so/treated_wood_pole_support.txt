Taageeraha tiirka alwaaxda la daweeyey
Qaybta looxeedka dhismaha
<&recipe>Taageeraha tiirka toosan ee alwaaxda la daweeyey wuxuu siinayaa hoolalka mashiinnada iyo looxyada marinnada taageero muuqata.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Rog oo dhig si uu u noqdo taageero muuqata oo u gaar ah tiirarka jiifka, tiirarka taagan iyo geesaha madallada.<br>
