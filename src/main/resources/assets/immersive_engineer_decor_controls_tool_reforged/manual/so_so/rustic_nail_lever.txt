Xakamaynta redstone
<&recipe>Qayb xakamayn oo yar waxay dhismayaasha warshadda siisaa meel fure redstone ah oo cad.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Midig guji si aad u kala beddesho wax-soo-saar buuxa oo wareegyada dhow loo diro.<br>
<br>
Ogeysiis: Wuxuu bixiyaa redstone awooddiisu buuxdo inta uu shidan yahay.
