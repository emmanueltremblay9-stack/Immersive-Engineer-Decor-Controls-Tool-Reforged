Qalabka burburinta gacanta
<&recipe>Dubbaha burburiya macdantu waa qalab gacanta ah oo birta iyo dahabka hore loogu farsameeyo.<br>
<br>
Ku isticmaal bir ceeriin, dahab ceeriin, macdan bir ama dahab iyo noocyada deepslate si aad u hesho budada Immersive Engineering. Dubbuhu adkaysi buu lumiyaa, shabagga ayuu ku noqdaa ilaa uu jabo, qodistu way xirtaa, bartilmaameedkana dib ayuu u tuuraa.
