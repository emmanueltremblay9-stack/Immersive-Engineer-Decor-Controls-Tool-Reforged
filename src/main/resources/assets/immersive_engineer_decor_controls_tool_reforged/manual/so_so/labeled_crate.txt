Sanduuqa summadaysan
Summadda kaydka ee wax laga beddeli karo
<&recipe>Sanduuqan summadaysan waxaa loo sameeyey kala-habaynta cad ee qolka kaydka. Looxiisa hore waxaa loo tifaftiri karaa sidii calaamad yar oo goob shaqo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Midig guji looxa summadda si aad wax uga beddesho summadda sanduuqa. Midig guji qaybta kale ee sanduuqa si aad u furto kaydka qaybaha, qalabka iyo sahayda dayactirka.<br>
