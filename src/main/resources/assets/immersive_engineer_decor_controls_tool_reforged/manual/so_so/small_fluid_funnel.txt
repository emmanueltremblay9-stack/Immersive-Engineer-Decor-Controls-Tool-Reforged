Maseelka dareeraha
Qalabka khadka dareeraha
<&recipe>Maseelka dareeraha ee yar waa qayb yar oo khadka dareeraha ah, loogu talagalay haamaha yaryar, marinnada tuubooyinka, meelaha ururinta iyo qulqulka redstone xakameeyo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Dhig meesha dareeraha lagu ururiyo hoosteeda, waxyaabaha ku kaydsanna u gudbi haamo ama tuubooyin hoose.<br>
