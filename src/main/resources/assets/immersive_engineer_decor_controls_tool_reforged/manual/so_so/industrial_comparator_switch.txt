Furaha isbarbardhigaha
Calaamadda ku xiran
<&recipe>Fure dareeme yar ah wuxuu akhriyaa baloogga uu ku rakiban yahay, akhriskaasna wuxuu u beddelaa wax-soo-saar looxeed oo fudud.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Ku rakib kaydadka, haamaha ama qalabka redstone. Marka baloogga ku xiran bixiyo wax-soo-saarka isbarbardhigaha, weelka ama redstone, furuhu wuxuu bixiyaa redstone awooddiisu buuxdo.<br>
<br>
Ogeysiis: U adeegso inaad heerarka kaydka ama fiilooyinka qarsoon u beddesho khadad xakamayn shid/dami oo cad.
