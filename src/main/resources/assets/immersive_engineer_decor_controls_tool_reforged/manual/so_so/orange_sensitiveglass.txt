Muraayadda xasaasiga ah ee oranji
Muraayadda calaamadda
<&recipe>Baloog muraayad ah oo redstone ka falceliya wuxuu ilaaliyaa aragtida isagoo muujinaya xaaladda calaamadda.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: U adeegso daaqadaha qolka xakamaynta, looxyada iftiimaya iyo muraayadda warshadda ee la ilaaliyo.<br>
<br>
Ogeysiis: Wuxuu xaaladda ku beddelaa awoodda redstone ee dhow.
