Marinnada
Marinnada socodka iyo taageerayaasha
Tiirarka birta adag, tiirarka alwaaxda la daweeyey, shabaqyada, marinnada socodka, biraha badbaadada, sallaanada, albaabbada, daaqadaha iyo dalool-daboolladu waxay sameeyaan jidad dayactir oo kooban gudaha warshadaha.<br>
<br>
Qaybaha marinnada birta adag waxay isticmaalaan saxanno bir adag ah. Samee ama dhig miiska farsamaynta biraha, geli dubbe Immersive Engineering ah, kaddibna biraha adag ee la shubay dubbe ugu beddel saxanno bir adag ah, si aad uga samayso tiirar, marinno socod, daaqado, albaabbo, biro badbaado iyo qurxin bir adag kale ah.<br>
<br>
Dhig meelaha mashiinnadu uga baahan yihiin marin dayactir, jidad sare, godad dayactir ama geeso ammaan badan.
