Cadaadiyaha ulaha Muslee
Cadaadiyaha cuntada
<&recipe>Madbacadda Muslee waxay cuntada caadiga ah u beddeshaa sahay yar.<br>
<br>
Hayso rooti, tufaax iyo iniin qamadi, qaraha, bocor, dabocase ama hemp warshadeed oo isticmaal madbacadda. Mid kasta ayay isticmaashaa, booska hubisaa, afar baar samaysaa, wayna dhaawacantaa.<br>
<br>
Haddii wax ama boos maqnaado, waxba lama isticmaalo.
