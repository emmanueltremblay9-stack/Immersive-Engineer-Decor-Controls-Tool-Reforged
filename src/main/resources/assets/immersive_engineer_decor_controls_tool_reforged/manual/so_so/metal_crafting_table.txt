Miiska farsamaynta biraha
Goobta samaynta saxannada
<&recipe>Miiska biruhu wuxuu leeyahay meel dubbe, shabag 3x3 ah iyo meel wax ka soo baxaan.<br>
<br>
U isticmaal sida miis caadi ah. Saxanka IE, dubbaha injineerka geli meesha qalabka, agabka shabagga, saxankana ka qaado meesha soo baxda.<br>
<br>
Qurxinta birta adag waxay isticmaashaa saxanno miiskan lagu sameeyey.
