Cabbiraha calaamadda
<&recipe>Cabbire yar oo qolka xakamaynta ah wuxuu xoogga redstone ku soo bandhigaa akhris looxeed oo cad.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Ku rakib halka awoodda redstone ee dhow hal mar la eegi karo.<br>
<br>
Ogeysiis: Wuxuu akhriyaa xoogga redstone ee dhow laga bilaabo 0 ilaa 15.
