Waalka hal-jihada ee tuubada dareeraha
Qalabka khadka dareeraha
<&recipe>Waalka hal-jihada ee dareeruhu waa qayb yar oo khadka dareeraha ah, loogu talagalay haamaha yaryar, marinnada tuubooyinka, meelaha ururinta iyo qulqulka redstone xakameeyo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Waalka la sim jihada qulqulka la doonayo.<br>
