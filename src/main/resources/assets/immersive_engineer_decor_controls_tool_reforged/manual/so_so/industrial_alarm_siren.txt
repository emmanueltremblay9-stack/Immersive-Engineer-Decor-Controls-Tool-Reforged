Hoonka digniinta
Jawaabta calaamadda
<&recipe>Seeriga digniintu waa tilmaame redstone oo lagu rakibo wejiga block. Xaaladda korontadu waxay raacdaa calaamadda block uu ku xiran yahay.<br>
<br>
Ku dheji weji adag oo koronto sii si aad ula socoto albaab, mashiin, digniin ama guddi.<br>
<br>
Seerigu wuxuu cusboonaadaa hal mar ilbiriqsigii inta calaamaddu shaqaynayso.
