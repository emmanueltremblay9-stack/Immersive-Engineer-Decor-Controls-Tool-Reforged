Mashiinka lisidda
Qalabka adeegga sagxadda shaqada
<&recipe>Mashiinka lisidda ee yar waa baloog adeeg goob-shaqo oo loogu talagalay farsamaynta, kaydinta, dhigista, jebinta, ururinta ama otomaatig yar.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Dhig lo’da agteeda, diyaarina hab lagu saaro weelasha caanaha ama dareeraha.<br>
