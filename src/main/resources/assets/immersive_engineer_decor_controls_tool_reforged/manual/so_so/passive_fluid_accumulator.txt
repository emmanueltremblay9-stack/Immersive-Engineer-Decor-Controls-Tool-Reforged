Dareeraha aan tamarta u baahnayn
Qalabka khadka dareeraha
<&recipe>Ururiyaha dareeraha ee aan tamarta u baahnayn waa qayb yar oo khadka dareeraha ah, loogu talagalay haamaha yaryar, marinnada tuubooyinka, meelaha ururinta iyo qulqulka redstone xakameeyo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Ku dhig ilaha biyaha ee ku habboon dhinacooda, meelnana u daa saarista dareeraha.<br>
