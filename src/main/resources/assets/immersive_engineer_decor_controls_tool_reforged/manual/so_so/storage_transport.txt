Kaydinta iyo raridda
Qaybaha kaydka iyo qulqulka
Qaybaha kaydinta iyo rariddu waxay maamulaan sanduuqyo, foostooyin, maseello iyo waalallo si warshadda dhexdeeda loogu gudbiyo si kooban.<br>
<br>
Bogaggan isticmaal markaad dejinayso qolalka kaydka, marinnada tuubooyinka, haamaha adeegga iyo khadadka dareeraha ee la xakameeyo.
<np>
Sanduuqa summadaysan
Summadda kaydka ee wax laga beddeli karo
<&labeled_crate>Sanduuqan summadaysan waxaa loo sameeyey kala-habaynta cad ee qolka kaydka. Looxiisa hore waxaa loo tifaftiri karaa sidii calaamad yar oo goob shaqo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Midig guji looxa summadda si aad wax uga beddesho summadda sanduuqa. Midig guji qaybta kale ee sanduuqa si aad u furto kaydka qaybaha, qalabka iyo sahayda dayactirka.<br>
<np>
Foostada dareeraha
Qalabka khadka dareeraha
<&fluid_barrel>Qaybtani waa qalab yar oo khadka dareeraha ah, loogu talagalay haamaha yaryar, marinnada tuubooyinka, meelaha ururinta iyo qulqulka redstone xakameeyo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Ku dhig meel tuubooyin ama baaldiyo ay gaari karaan wejiga haanta.<br>
<np>
Maseelka dareeraha
Qalabka khadka dareeraha
<&small_fluid_funnel>Maseelka dareeraha ee yar waa qayb yar oo khadka dareeraha ah, loogu talagalay haamaha yaryar, marinnada tuubooyinka, meelaha ururinta iyo qulqulka redstone xakameeyo.<br>
<br>
Isticmaal: Dhig meesha dareeraha lagu ururiyo hoosteeda, waxyaabaha ku kaydsanna u gudbi haamo ama tuubooyin hoose.<br>
