Mashiinnada goobta shaqada
Mashiinnada iyo gelinta/bixitaanka goobta shaqada
Mashiinnada goobta shaqadu waxay hal baloog gudaheed ku bixiyaan farsamayn, dhaqaajinta alaabta, dhalaalin, baabi’in, ururin iyo adeegyo kale oo kooban.<br>
<br>
Fur bogagga soo socda si aad u hesho habka farsamaynta mashiin kasta, sawirkiisa, macluumaadka sheekada iyo tilmaamaha isticmaalka wax-ku-oolka ah.
