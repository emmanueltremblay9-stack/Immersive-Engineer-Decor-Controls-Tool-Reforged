Skwychell Komparyer
Arwodh junys
<&recipe>Skwychell sensour gompakt a red an stock may yw gorrys hag a dreyl an redyans na dhe eskorrans panel sempel.<br>
<br>
Devnydh: gor hi war rolasow, tankys po daffar rudhven. Pan dhiskwedh an stock junys eskorrans komparyer, lestr po rudhven, an skwychell a ry rudhven a leun-grevder.<br>
<br>
Notennow: usya hi rag treyl nivelyow gwithva po gwivrennow kudh dhe lynennow reoli kler gweythrek/diweythrek.
