Derbynner Kevren Analog
Reolyans kevrennys
<&recipe>Derbynner kevrennys a ry amkan pell gweladow dhe banellow reoli.<br>
<br>
Devnydh: gwra klikya gans Perl an Diwedh rag gul perl kevrennys. Usya an perl na hag ow skolkya rag weythenni an derbynner a-bell.<br>
<br>
Notennow: an derbynner ma a ry arwodh rudhven a leun-grevder pan y'n weythennir.
