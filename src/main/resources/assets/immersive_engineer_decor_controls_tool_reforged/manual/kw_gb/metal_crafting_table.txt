Moos Kreftya Metal
Gorsav oberi platyow
<&recipe>Bank ober metal gans spas morthwyl arbennik, grid grefti 3x3 ha spas dhe-ves.<br>
<br>
Devnydh: usya an gril kepar ha moos kreftya normal. Rag furvya platyow IE, gor Morthol an Enjynour y'n slot daffar ha danvez an plat y'n gril; ena kemmer an plat furvys a'n eskorrans.<br>
<br>
Afinansow dur a dhevnydh platyow dur. Ynwor morthol Immersive Engineering, gwask etewigow dur gans an morthol rag gul platyow dur, ena usya an platyow rag kreftya peulyow, hensyow kerdhes, fenestriow, darasow, balustransow hag afinansow dur erel.<br>
