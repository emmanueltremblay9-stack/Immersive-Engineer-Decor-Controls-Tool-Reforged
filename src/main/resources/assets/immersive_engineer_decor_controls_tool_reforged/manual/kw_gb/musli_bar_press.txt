Gwasker Barryow Muslee
Gwasker boos
<&recipe>An pres barr Muslee a wra kevra rannow boos kompak dhyworth boos kemmyn.<br>
<br>
Devnydh: Kemmer bara, aval ha has gwaneth, melon, pompyon, bet po Industrial Hemp, ha devnydh an pres. Presyans sewen a dhevor unn a bub tra, a wir bos spas, a wra peswar barr Muslee Melange hag a goll hirder an pres.<br>
<br>
Mar peu tra fyllys po nag eus spas y'n inventori, ny vydh tra devorys.
