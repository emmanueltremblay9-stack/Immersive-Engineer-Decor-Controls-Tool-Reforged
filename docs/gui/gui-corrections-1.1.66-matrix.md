## Deferred Tree Cutter contract

- One operation is charged once for the whole tree, with total FE increasing with counted logs. The FE coefficient, capacity limit, work duration, and maximum tree bound remain unselected.
- After work completes, logs and leaves are felled progressively from top to bottom. The machine collects logs only; leaves are destroyed normally and their ordinary drops land in the world.
- The operation must be bounded to one tree, avoid loading chunks, revalidate protection and output capacity before mutation, and resume safely after reload without duplicating or losing drops.
- All 27 legacy inventory positions must remain preserved and recoverable. Behavior when one visible output cannot hold the logs, including mixed species and stack-size limits, remains unresolved.
- No Tree Fell gameplay or accepted Tree Cutter visual change is part of this batch.
