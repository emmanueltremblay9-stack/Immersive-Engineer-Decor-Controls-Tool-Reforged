# Immersive Engineer Decor&Controls&Tool Reforged 1.1.53-reconstructed

This release closes the seven gameplay, documentation, support, and provenance regressions reported against the public 1.1.41 build.

## Corrected

- Engineer's Manual integration no longer treats the Coarse Iron Grit and Coarse Gold Grit smelting recipes as crafting widgets. The complete resource validator covers 223 manual entries and 424 named widgets.
- The Small Solar Panel actively outputs to a receiver below it or on an allowed horizontal face, with one shared limit of 256 FE per tick and conservation accounting.
- The Metal Sliding Door and Iron Hatch use their intended metallic open/close sounds and game events.
- The custom Creative tab exposes the public player-facing blocks, machines, components, doors, hatches, solar panel, and tools.
- Conventional mining tags cover normal blocks: pickaxe for metal/stone, axe for wood, and shovel for soil. RETIA/REDIA is not an artificial requirement.
- MIT licensing, notices, credits, and reconstruction provenance are present at the repository root and inside the runtime JAR.
- GitHub Issues is the public support and bug-report channel.

## Validated compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
