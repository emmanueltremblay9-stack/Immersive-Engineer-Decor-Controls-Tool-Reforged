# Immersive Engineer Decor&Controls&Tool Reforged 1.1.55-reconstructed

This patch release corrects GitHub issues #8, #9, and #10 while preserving the existing Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge compatibility line.

## Corrected

- The iron bulb and inset lights now face back toward the clicked surface, and the iron floor and ceiling edge lights follow the player's horizontal look. Their outline geometry rotates from model-matched literal shapes instead of a generic support-mounted plate.
- The steel framed window is again a centered, independently surviving pane. Its axis follows the player's view and aligns with adjacent windows; removing an unrelated support no longer destroys it.
- The Decor and Defense sign item definitions changed from volumetric world-model parents to flat `item/generated` front-texture models, removing the inherited block-model side geometry associated with the report.

## Regression coverage

- One obsolete generic support-removal GameTest was replaced with seven targeted required GameTests covering both light placement families, literal rotated light-shape oracles, light support removal, steel-window shape, neighbor-aware placement, and independent survival.
- The required NeoForge GameTest suite increased from 182 to 188 tests.
- `validateSignItemModels` is part of `check` and verifies the sign item parent, front texture, absence of volumetric elements, GUI lighting, and display transforms.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- Java 21
- NeoForge 21.1.230 minimum; qualification also covers 21.1.248
- Immersive Engineering 12.4.2-194 or newer compatible 1.21.1 build (required)
- JEI 19.32.0.359 or newer compatible 1.21.1 build (optional); qualification also covers 19.44.0.406

Engineer's Decor, Engineer's Tools, and Redstone Gauges and Switches are incorporated source projects, not dependencies to install. Their provenance is recorded in the project description, `CREDITS.md`, and `NOTICE.md`.
